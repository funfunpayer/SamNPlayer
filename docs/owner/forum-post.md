## Long version (EroScripts and similar forums)

**Title:** SamNPlayer — a free player, script generator and editor built around the Sam Neo 2 (and more than just The Handy)

Hi everyone,

I'm funfunpayer. I want to introduce a project I've been building: **SamNPlayer**.

**Why I built it**

Most players and generators out there are made with The Handy and other strokers in mind. I own a **Sam Neo 2**, and it works differently: it has vibration and suction, not a stroke. Playing a normal script on it never felt right. Getting a script for a video meant juggling several programs and websites.

So I wanted one system that does all of it in one place, made for the Sam Neo 2 first and still good for other devices. It is free.

**What it does**

- **Player.** Plays a video with its script and sends it to your device. A video brings its script along when the script sits next to it with the same name. You also get:
  - a heatmap;
  - lanes for every channel;
  - chapters and bookmarks;
  - a sleep timer and playlists.
- **Generator.** Makes a script from a video on your own computer. It follows the actual motion in the picture, and you can check and fix the result right there. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **Editor.** Drag, add or delete points on every channel, with undo and redo. There are range tools (speed cap, scale, invert), multi-axis files and *Optimize for* a device.
- **Sam Neo 2 feel.** You set how the suction follows the motion per scene: *Oral*, *Intercourse*, *Breast* or *Hand*. A slider sets the mix between vibration and suction, and SamNPlayer can suggest a feel for each chapter. Leave it off, and the classic feel stays as it is.
- **Training.** Guided sessions (warm-up, build, hold, rest) with a real pause.
- **Scene Map.** The script's chapters shown as scenes, with thumbnails.

**Devices**

- **Sam Neo 2:** direct, vibration and suction.
- **The Handy, Lovense and most other toys:** through Intiface Central. Vibrators can even get their vibration from the video's sound.
- **OSR2 / SR6:** TCode over USB or Wi-Fi, including the extra axes.
- **Strokers:** a safety range, and a soft start after a jump in the video, so the device doesn't slam.

The app only shows what your device can actually do.

**Free**

SamNPlayer is free. No account, no subscription, no locked features. Donations are welcome but never required.

**Honest status**

This is version 0.7.0, and it's still young.

- It runs on Windows and Linux.
- Everything is tested automatically, including a run over 758 public community scripts. Real hardware testing so far is mostly my own Sam Neo 2.
- Generated scripts are a strong start, not a guaranteed perfect result. Please check them in the editor before you trust them.

I would really like feedback, especially:

- from people with other devices (Handy, Lovense, OSR / SR6);
- comparisons with the tools you use now, on the same video;
- bugs: there is a log under Tools → Log that helps me a lot.
